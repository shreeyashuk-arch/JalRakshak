# JalRakshak: Known Limitations & Prototype Boundaries

**Project:** JalRakshak — Predict the Failure. Protect the Water.  
**Hardware Platform:** Nordic Semiconductor nRF54LM20 DK  
**Role:** Prototype Research & Hackster.io "Sustain the System" Competition Demonstration

To maintain absolute scientific and engineering integrity, this document explicitly lists all current technical limitations and operational boundaries of the JalRakshak system.

---

## 1. Prototype Status & Non-Certification

> [!CAUTION]
> **Not Validated for Municipal or Life-Critical Deployment:**  
> JalRakshak is an experimental prototype research platform. None of the algorithms, composite reliability scores, or estimated maintenance windows are currently certified or validated for safety-critical municipal water authority deployment.

- All scores (e.g., *JalRakshak Water Reliability Score*) are experimental composite indicators.
- Maintenance windows (e.g., *"18–30 operating hours"*) are algorithmic estimates based on synthetic degradation gradients and must not be used as guaranteed operational guarantees.

---

## 2. Sensor Instrumentation & Mock Acquisition

1. **Current Absence of Physical Transducers:**
   - The current repository code utilizes a modular hardware abstraction layer (`firmware/src/sensors/sensor_manager.c`). In this initial milestone, sensor acquisition runs in software simulation/mock mode.
   - Physical I2C/SPI drivers for the ADXL355 accelerometer, 1-Wire DS18B20 drivers, and SAADC motor current acquisition drivers will be linked during Phase 2 physical bench testing.
2. **Environmental Noise & Calibration:**
   - Real-world borehole pump installations experience cavitation shocks, pipe resonance harmonics, and solar irradiance fluctuations that synthetic datasets cannot completely simulate.

---

## 3. Edge AI & Machine Learning Limitations

1. **Synthetic Training Data Baseline:**
   - The baseline classifier (`edge_ai/models/baseline_pump_classifier.joblib`) was trained on 2,000 synthetic time-series windows generated by `synthetic_generator.py`.
   - While the physical mathematical equations accurately represent bearing shock waves and electrical overload curves, the model has not yet seen real mechanical bearing flaking or impeller erosion datasets.
2. **Edge Hardware Acceleration:**
   - The embedded C implementation (`firmware/src/ai/edge_ai_engine.c`) currently executes feature calculation and decision scoring on the ARM Cortex-M33 application core.
   - Axon NPU hardware deployment requires the specialized Nordic Edge AI toolchain and quantization conversion, which is scheduled for the next physical iteration.

---

## 4. Wireless & AquaMesh Boundaries

1. **Mesh Simulation vs. Physical RF:**
   - The repository implements the official Bluetooth Mesh v1.1 packet structures and state machine in C (`firmware/src/bluetooth/aquamesh.c`).
   - Radio frequency mesh multi-hop communication is currently verified at the packet and payload layout level; physical 2.4 GHz propagation testing across geographically dispersed nodes requires physical nRF54LM20 DK hardware.
2. **Technician Gateway Handshake:**
   - Data mule synchronization currently relies on simulated BLE connection callbacks and ring-buffer flushing (`firmware/src/telemetry/telemetry.c`).
